# Opportunity313 ticketing and school verification — September 23, 2026

Parents and youth open **Event Tickets** near the top of Profile. Ticket pages retain a visible header while reloading or scrolling. An event shows its date, venue, student/guest options, and bus-route directions. Checkout holds spots for five minutes with a live countdown, then confirms a ticket with an on-device QR code. Unused tickets may be cancelled before the event. The existing demo events and tickets remain labeled as demos.

A student or parent selects a school from the youth profile and takes one photo of the school ID. The app checks that the photo contains readable text, then discards it on the device; the image is never uploaded or saved. This sends an in-app request to that school's ticket admins. The **Requests** tab shows a pending-count badge and a queue restricted to admins assigned to that school. An admin checks the student's existing name and grade against the school's own roster, then approves or rejects the link. The roster itself is not copied into Opportunity313. Once approved, the school link is retained for future student tickets; no photo or new approval is needed per purchase. Changing the youth's name or grade resets verification and requires a new photo request and roster check. The pending badge refreshes while the admin uses the app, when they reopen it, or when they refresh the list; remote push notifications and direct school-system connections remain later work.

A **student** allocation requires the ticket owner to select a verified youth profile for the event's specific school. The server enforces the school match when placing a hold, issuing the ticket, and checking it in. A youth can have only one active hold or issued ticket per event. Student ticket codes are not offered through the app's Share button. **General/guest** allocations still work without a school link. Older student tickets with no assigned, verified youth are refused at check-in under the new rule; their owners need a new valid reservation. The app no longer asks staff to inspect IDs at the event gate.

Platform admins assign confirmed staff accounts to schools in the **Ticketing** tab. A school's ticket admin controls that school's event seat limit, available student/guest spots, and reservation availability. Other assigned event staff can scan tickets but cannot change inventory. Venue seating is separate; only a platform admin can change a shared venue's physical limit. Seat changes require a reason and are audited. Direct client writes to venues, events, and allocations are disabled, and the old one-step reservation endpoint is unavailable.

Event and opportunity detail screens include **Bus Routes to This Event**. A parent or student may enter a starting address or grant when-in-use location access. **View Bus Routes & Transfers** opens Apple Maps transit directions; refreshing location updates the starting point for the next route request. Opportunity313 does not run its own live bus feed. Map coverage depends on Apple Maps, and opportunities without a street address should be confirmed before traveling.
